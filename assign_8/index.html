What is Bike Insurance?

Bike insurance is a policy that provides financial protection against losses or damages involving your motorcycle or scooter. Depending on the policy, it can also provide protection against third-party liabilities.

Important: Third-party motor insurance is generally required by law for vehicles used on public roads in India. Check your policy terms and applicable regulations for the latest requirements.

Types of Bike Insurance

Third-Party Insurance

Provides cover for legal liability arising from injury, death or property damage caused to a third party, subject to policy terms and applicable limits.

Comprehensive Insurance

Combines third-party liability cover with protection for your own bike against covered risks such as accidents, theft, fire and natural calamities.

Own Damage Cover

Provides protection for your bike against covered accidental damage and other specified risks. It is generally purchased along with mandatory third-party cover.

Common Coverage

  • Accidental damage to the insured bike
  • Theft of the insured bike
  • Damage caused by fire or explosion
  • Damage caused by certain natural or man-made events
  • Third-party liability, subject to applicable terms
  • Optional add-ons such as zero depreciation, roadside assistance and engine protection, depending on the insurer and policy

Common Exclusions

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Driving without a valid licence
  • Use of the bike in violation of policy conditions
  • Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances, subject to applicable law and policy terms
  • Mechanical or electrical breakdowns unless specifically covered

What Affects Your Premium?

Factor Impact
Bike Model & Engine Capacity Higher-value or higher-capacity bikes may have different premiums.
Bike Age The age and depreciation of the vehicle can affect the premium.
Location Risk levels and applicable insurance rates can vary by location.
Claim History Eligible claim-free years may affect your No Claim Bonus.
Add-ons Optional add-ons generally increase the premium.

How to Make a Bike Insurance Claim

  1. Inform your insurance company as soon as possible after the incident.
  2. Register a police complaint where required, especially in theft or major accident cases.
  3. Provide the requested documents and incident details.
  4. Allow the insurer's inspection or survey process when applicable.
  5. Get repairs done according to the insurer's claim process and policy conditions.
  6. Keep invoices, receipts and other supporting documents safely.

Documents Commonly Required

  • Bike insurance policy details
  • Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Valid driving licence
  • Claim form
  • Identity and address proof, where required
  • FIR/police report for applicable cases
  • Repair estimates and bills, where applicable

Insurance Overview

Motor vehicle insurance provides financial protection against certain losses, damages and third-party liabilities arising from the use of a vehicle. The exact protection depends on the type of policy, vehicle, insurer, add-ons and policy conditions.

Important: Third-party motor insurance is generally mandatory for vehicles used on public roads in India. Always check the latest applicable regulations and your policy wording.

Vehicle Insurance Categories

Private / Personal Use

Car Insurance

Car insurance can protect private cars against covered own-damage risks and third-party liabilities.

  • Accident damage
  • Theft
  • Fire and specified natural events
  • Third-party liability
  • Optional add-on protection
Commercial Vehicle

Heavy Load Vehicle Insurance

Commercial vehicle insurance is designed for vehicles used for transporting goods or carrying heavy loads, subject to the vehicle's registration and permitted use.

  • Trucks
  • Heavy goods vehicles
  • Multi-axle vehicles
  • Goods carriers
  • Other eligible commercial vehicles

Types of Motor Insurance

Third-Party Liability

Covers eligible legal liabilities arising from injury, death or property damage caused to third parties, subject to applicable laws, limits and policy terms.

Comprehensive / Package Policy

Generally combines third-party liability with own-damage protection for covered events such as accidents, theft, fire and specified natural or man-made events.

Own Damage Cover

Provides protection for the insured vehicle against covered accidental damage and other specified risks. It is generally used together with mandatory third-party cover.

Common Coverage for Cars

  • Accidental damage to the insured car
  • Theft of the vehicle
  • Fire and explosion, according to policy terms
  • Specified natural calamities
  • Specified man-made events
  • Third-party liability
  • Optional add-ons such as zero depreciation and roadside assistance, if available

Common Coverage for Heavy Load Vehicles

  • Accidental damage to the commercial vehicle
  • Theft of the insured vehicle
  • Fire and specified natural events
  • Third-party liability
  • Coverage options based on commercial vehicle usage
  • Selected add-ons available from the insurer
  • Protection subject to declared vehicle use, permits and policy conditions
Commercial vehicle note: Heavy vehicles may require valid registration, permits, fitness certificates, appropriate driving licences and other legally required documents. Insurance coverage can be affected when policy or legal requirements are not followed.

What Can Affect Your Premium?

  • Vehicle make, model and age
  • Insured Declared Value (IDV), where applicable
  • Engine capacity and vehicle specifications
  • Vehicle registration and usage location
  • Type of vehicle and its intended use
  • Previous claim history and eligible No Claim Bonus
  • Selected coverage and optional add-ons
  • For commercial vehicles, usage, carrying capacity and other risk factors

Common Exclusions

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Mechanical or electrical breakdowns unless specifically covered
  • Driving without a valid licence
  • Use outside the permitted policy conditions
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances, subject to applicable law and policy terms
  • Consequential losses that are not covered by the policy

How to Make an Insurance Claim

  1. Inform the insurer promptly after an accident, theft or other covered incident.
  2. Contact the police when required, especially for theft or serious accidents.
  3. Provide the claim form and required vehicle and incident details.
  4. Submit photographs, reports and other supporting evidence when requested.
  5. Allow the insurer's survey/inspection process where applicable.
  6. Use the insurer's approved repair or claim process.
  7. Keep all repair estimates, invoices and receipts.

Commonly Required Documents

For Cars

  • Insurance policy details
  • Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Valid driving licence
  • Claim form
  • Identity/address proof, where required
  • FIR or police report, where applicable
  • Repair estimates and bills

For Heavy Vehicles

  • Insurance policy details
  • Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Valid commercial driving licence
  • Permit, where applicable
  • Fitness certificate, where applicable
  • Claim form
  • FIR/police report, where applicable
  • Repair estimates and invoices

Tips Before Buying Insurance

  • Compare the coverage, not just the premium.
  • Check the policy's exclusions, deductibles and limits.
  • Review the Insured Declared Value where applicable.
  • Choose add-ons based on your actual requirements.
  • For commercial vehicles, ensure the policy matches the permitted vehicle use.
  • Keep registration, licence, permit and other required documents valid.
  • Read the policy wording before purchasing or renewing.
